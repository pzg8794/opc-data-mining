At the end of the first week I will set up individual blogs for each student.  The instructions for the individual blog are fairly straightforward.

  1. You need to post a short paragraph on what you have been doing on data cleaning/preparation in general or for the course in particular. Some examples of things that students in past courses have felt like posting: their view of their discussions with their teammates, any interesting thoughts after reading a chapter in a textbook or in a paper, and so on.
  2. Two blog postings are required each week, and must be distributed over the week. While I would like to see a posting on a Tuesday and Friday typically, other schedules are possible and you can adopt one that is convenient to you. [Note: several postings on the same day count as only one item toward the blog score.]  Don't omit your blog posts!  They count toward your final grade.
  3. Note: the blog is a private and confidential way to exchange information between each student and the instructor. No other student gets to read your blog. If you do want to share some thoughts about course-related issues with other students, use the Lounge area in General Discussions.
  4. Finally, if you have private questions for me, feel free to use the blog. I read the blogs regularly.
